PRODUCT IN ONE SENTENCE
-----------------------
OJT Intern Path is a multi-tenant web platform that connects schools, companies,
municipal halls (LGU hosts), and student interns for internship discovery,
school endorsement, host hiring, exclusive placement (one active accept),
school–company agreements (MOAs), OJT hour logging / DTR, assessments, KPIs,
reports, and certificates — with subscription billing via PayMongo.

3. TWO ROLE LAYERS (READ THIS BEFORE CHANGING ACCESS)
================================================================================

There are two different "role" concepts. Most access bugs come from treating
them as one.

A) APP WORKSPACE ROLE
  User.role / User::effectiveAppRole() includes:
    admin | school | company | municipal_hall | student | guest | null
  This chooses the workspace shell and the route's requiresRole.

  effectiveAppRole() (backend/app/Models/User.php):
    - platform admin → admin
    - school-org membership with slug intern or student_member → student
    - school-org membership with no role slug, if the user has a student row → student
    - EVERY other school-org membership → school
      (School Admin, Department Head, Teacher, Registrar, custom subuser)
    - any company-org OR municipal_hall-org membership → company / municipal_hall
      (municipal_hall reuses company workspace)
    - otherwise users.role, with guest stored as null

  Post-login landing (src/router/index.ts getRoleDashboard):
    admin          → /admin/overview
    student        → /intern   (product language: Intern; API/DB role often "student")
    guest          → /guest
    school         → first school nav page the membership permissions allow, else /settings
    company /
    municipal_hall → first company nav page the membership permissions allow, else /settings
                     (navRole for municipal_hall = company)

  Pending org verification (school | company | municipal_hall):
    forced to /organization-verification until approved/verified.

  There is no ojt_admin slug and no "OJT Office" role. "CvSU OJT Office" is the
  display name of the primary school account (school@cvsu.edu.ph). Its membership
  role is School Admin.

B) ORGANIZATION MEMBERSHIP ROLE
  organization_memberships.role_id points at a tenant role.
  Effective permissions = role permission keys ∪ grants − denies.
  GET /api/auth/me returns them on memberships[0].permissions.
  The frontend reads memberships[0] only (same as hasPermission()).
  After a permission save, the subuser must log out and back in so /auth/me
  reloads that list.

GLOBAL SEEDED SLUGS (RbacSeeder) vs TENANT DEFAULT SLUGS (TenantRoleService)
  Platform: system_admin, platform_super_admin, platform_support_admin
  School global: school_admin, school_department_head, student_member
  Company global: company_admin, company_hr_manager

  New school tenants also get (TenantRoleService::defaultDefinitionsForType):
    school_admin, department_head, teacher, registrar, intern
  New company / municipal tenants also get:
    company_admin, manager, hr, finance

  The demo department head uses the GLOBAL slug school_department_head,
  not the tenant slug department_head. Do not merge or rename those slugs
  unless a task explicitly says so.

DEFAULT SCHOOL ROLE PACKS (OrgPermissions + TenantRoleService)
  School Admin: full org catalog, including manage students, roles, subscription,
    internships, agreements, applications, OJT, reports, export, certificates.
  Department Head: view/approve applications, view students, view/approve OJT,
    reports including export, agreements, certificates, assessments, profile.
    No manage_students, no manage_roles, no manage_subscription.
  Teacher: org.view_students, org.view_ojt_progress, org.view_applications,
    org.view_reports, org.reports_view, view_reports, upload_files, manage_profile.
    manage_profile does NOT grant course-hour management or org required-hours edits.
  Registrar: view/manage students, reports, manage_users, upload_files.
  Intern (membership slug intern): upload_files only, and effectiveAppRole is student.

DEFAULT COMPANY ROLE PACKS
  Company Admin: members, roles, subscription, internships, agreements,
    applications, approve OJT logs, assessments, reports, export, certificates.
    Does not include org.view_students (that is a school roster key).
  HR: internships, agreements, applications, approve OJT, assessments, reports.
    No roles, no subscription.
  Manager: internships, applications, reports (narrower than HR).
  Finance: agreements, reports, subscription.

PERMISSION CATALOG (canonical org.* keys)

  Approve:  org.approve_applications, org.approve_agreements, org.approve_ojt_logs,

6. CORE DOMAIN FLOWS
================================================================================

A) INTERNSHIP / APPLICATION PIPELINE
  Company (or municipal hall) creates the posting in the UI (eligible courses,
  skills, duration text). School-hosted internships exist on the API; there is
  no school "create posting" screen.
  Student applies → status submitted (blocked if active placement — see 6A2).
  School endorses or school-rejects. Endorsement is required BEFORE interview
  and BEFORE company acceptance. API permission: org.approve_applications.
  Interview is optional and a separate record. The proposer cannot confirm
  their own proposal.
  Application tracker stages (ApplicationTracker service):
    applied → endorsed → interview_scheduled → interviewed → assessed → accepted
    Terminal / special: rejected, withdrawn_placed_elsewhere,
    placement_cancelled | placement_declined | placement_ended
  Company accepts or rejects only after endorsement → accepted | rejected.
  OJT logs exist only after accepted.
  Duplicate application guard and duplicate agreement guard are in place.
  School.vue endorsement confirm snapshots the decision locally before await.
  AlertDialog emits action then close; reading pendingDecision after close
  used to throw "Cannot read properties of null (reading 'action')".

A2) PLACEMENT EXCLUSIVITY (ONE ACTIVE ACCEPT) — ADDED 2026-11-03
  Rule: an intern with an active placement cannot apply to or be accepted by
  any other company or municipal hall.

  Active placement =
    application status/tracker Accepted at a company OR municipal host,
    AND not cancelled / declined / ended / completed,
    AND applications.active_placement_key = student_id (unique constraint).
  An intern can have at most one active placement.

  On host accept (same DB transaction via PlacementExclusivityService):
    - lock student row (lockForUpdate) + unique active_placement_key
    - set all other open apps to withdrawn_placed_elsewhere
      (status + tracker_stage; withdrawal_reason "Placed elsewhere";
       do NOT delete)
    - cancel scheduled interviews with other hosts
    - notify intern + each affected host
    - concurrent second accept → ConflictHttpException / clear error

  Apply endpoints (jobs + municipal programs share POST /api/applications):
    reject with 422 code active_placement + message naming the host/role.
  Accept of already-placed intern: blocked (conflict / validation).

  UI: FindInternships / InternshipDetail / Intern apply controls show
      "Already placed" + reason when active placement exists
      (useActivePlacement / GET /api/student/active-placement).

  Hosts and school see withdrawn applicants with label
  "Withdrawn — placed elsewhere" (not silent disappearance).
  School endorsements page has a "Withdrawn — placed elsewhere" section.

  Closing / releasing placement:
    POST /api/applications/{id}/placement/close  body: { reason }
    Reasons: cancelled | declined | ended | completed
    Who can close:
      Host (company/municipal) + School: cancelled, ended, completed
      Admin: all of the above
      Intern: declined only
    After close: active_placement_key cleared; intern may apply again.
    Withdrawn apps STAY withdrawn (not restored).

  Audit only (do not auto-fix multi-accepted legacy rows):
    php artisan placements:audit-multi-accepted

  Key files:
    backend/app/Services/PlacementExclusivityService.php
    backend/app/Services/ApplicationTracker.php
    backend/database/migrations/2026_11_03_180000_add_placement_exclusivity_to_applications.php
    src/composables/useActivePlacement.ts (or equivalent)
    src/services/applications.ts (activePlacement / closePlacement)

  Unpaid status=pending orgs get FREE limits until verify (SubscriptionPlanService)
  Active orgs keep current plan during checkout.
  If PAYMONGO_SECRET_KEY is empty, checkout and verify return HTTP 422
  code paymongo_not_configured BEFORE any subscription mutation.
  The UI shows an info dialog and refreshes. It does not fake success, skip
  verify, or invent a mock provider. Do not add a fake payment path.
  Host org.verified / subscription.write middleware can 402 new host actions
  when subscription/verification is missing.

K) SCHOOL STUDENT ACCOUNTS
  /school/students — temporary student logins, roster, setup links.
  Visible only with student-manage keys (see section 4).
  Students are school-provisioned; they use the school subscription code.
  student_id_number / studentNumber is REQUIRED on school create (and unique
  within the school). Validation: App\Support\StudentNumber + src/utils/studentNumber.ts.
  Students generally cannot change an existing number; may fill once if missing
  (ProfileController rules).

L) ORGANIZATION VERIFICATION
  School, company, and municipal_hall users with verificationStatus pending,
  under_review, or rejected are forced to /organization-verification until
  approved/verified (router needsOrganizationVerification).
  Sub-accounts inherit verification from parent org via formatUserProfile.
  Do not weaken verification.

M) FORCED PASSWORD CHANGE
  mustChangePassword still goes to Change Password, then
  authStore.refreshUser(), then the role dashboard.
  Do not skip that refresh (P0-01).

  └─ school course → skill category defaults (settings)

Agreement (MOA between school & company orgs)
